What the name means

  • No feeds.Nothing decides what comes next for you. Every story, game and sheet is picked by a person.
  • No ads.Nothing sold to your child and nothing tracking them. You pay for the thing itself.
  • It ends.Every activity has a last page. When it's done, it's done, and the screen goes back down.
