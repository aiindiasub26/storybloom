Privacy & refunds

Last updated 9 November 2026

StoryBloom is made by a parent for children aged 3–8. It is designed to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • From parents: your name, email and/or WhatsApp number, and, if you tell us, your child's age and your country. We use these only to send your code, renewal reminders and early-access news.
  • Payments: handled by Ziina. We never see or store your card details. We receive a payment reference and the amount.
  • Your devices: a random device number, so a code can be used on up to 3 devices.

What we don't collect

  • No child accounts and no information about your child inside the app.
  • Stars, stickers, recordings and progress stay on your device and are never sent to us.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, no selling or sharing your details.

Your choices

Ask us any time to see, correct or delete your details: DM @unscrolled.uae or reply to any of our emails.

Refunds

If StoryBloom isn't right for your family, message us within 7 days of paying and we'll refund you in full. Your code is switched off when the refund is made.

Renewals

StoryBloom is paid once a year. There is no automatic charge. We remind you before your year ends and you choose whether to renew.
